INTRODUCTION

Building Beyond 100 Years

A new business that lasts beyond three years depends on luck and the skill of the entrepreneur; beyond twenty years, it depends on structure, systems, processes, and discipline; to reach beyond 100 years, it depends on core values and core purpose.

Investment Wisdom

Over the past 50 years, my strategy to build wealth changed from speculation to investment. Speculation requires quick instincts and carries high risk; few survive. My strategy in investment is to look for value, place a thoughtful bet, and let time do the heavy lifting while enjoying life with less stress.

Cash as Oxygen

Cash is not king; cash is oxygen! If a business is dead, cash does not help. Maintain zero or manageable debt, keep liquidity, and wait for the right opportunities when market cycles turn.
